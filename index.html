Why people stall

Almost nobody gets hurt in Dubai off-plan because they picked the wrong city.

They get hurt on timing, on developer choice, and on buying a render they never stress tested. Six versions of the same problem.

01

You are on nine broker lists and all nine say the same launch is the one

Volume is not a shortlist. When every inbox says urgent, nothing in there has been matched to your budget, your timeline or your risk.

02

You are buying the render, not the developer

Off-plan risk is mostly developer risk. The delivery record sits behind the marketing, and it is the part that decides whether you get keys on time.

03

The payment plan looks generous and you cannot tell what it is hiding

Post-handover terms can be a real advantage or a way to make a thin deal look comfortable. You need to read which one is in front of you.

04

You are overseas and nobody has walked you through how it actually completes

Reservation, escrow, payment schedule, handover. Most of my buyers never fly in for the purchase, but only because the process was explained before they committed.

05

Free content never looks at your money

My videos are free and they are useful. They still cannot see your capital, your timeline, your financing or the deal sitting in your inbox this week.

06

Dubai or Abu Dhabi, and no one will give you a straight answer

The honest answer depends on your time horizon and what you want the asset to do. That is a conversation, not a headline.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before applying

How is this different from watching your YouTube or booking the free 15 minute call?

The videos teach you how off-plan works and how I assess it. The free 15 minute call on offplanjosh.com is a short introduction where we see if there is a fit for a brokerage conversation around current launches. Both are useful and both stay free.

This seat is different in one specific way. It is structured, application gated consulting time on your situation. Scheduled calls, messaging between them, and your budget, timeline and shortlist reviewed properly across a full year rather than in a quarter of an hour.

Do you only work with off-plan investors, or also home buyers?

Both. Off-plan is where I spend most of my time and where I can add the most, but plenty of people come to me buying a home to live in and wanting to make sure they are not overpaying for the wrong product in the wrong community. If ready stock suits you better than off-plan, I will tell you that.

Is this investment or securities advice?

No. This is educational consulting about property decisions in Dubai and the wider UAE. It is not securities advice, it is not regulated financial advice, and it is not portfolio management. You make your own decisions and you are responsible for them. Take independent tax and legal advice for your own jurisdiction.

Will you guarantee a yield or a purchase outcome?

No, and nobody honest will. Property involves capital at risk. Outcomes depend on your capital, your timing, the product you pick, the developer delivering, your financing and local rules, and several of those sit outside my control and yours.

What I commit to is process. We review your brief, we work the shortlist logic together, we get on the calls, and I reply in messaging. That is the promise.

What happens after I apply?

I read the application myself. If it looks like a fit, we book a call and go through where you are, what you are working with and what you are trying to achieve. If it is not a fit, I will tell you and point you at the free routes instead. Nobody is signed up on the spot.

Do I get messaging with you?

Yes, messaging access is part of the seat, with replies within 30 minutes. Launches move quickly and a good window can open and close between scheduled calls, so waiting a week to ask about something you saw on Tuesday does not work.

Is this one to one, or am I sharing the calls with other people?

One to one. The calls are between you and me, and they are about your situation, your numbers and your shortlist. You are not sitting in a room waiting for your turn, and you are not getting a general answer that had to work for twelve other people at once.

I am not in the UAE. Can I still do this?

Yes. Most of my buyers purchase without flying in, and the remote process is one of the things we cover properly on the calls. Reservation, escrow, the payment schedule and handover all get mapped before you commit to anything.
